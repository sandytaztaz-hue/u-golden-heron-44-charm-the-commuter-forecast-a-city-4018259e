One line of weather forecast, at most 140 characters, for a city that does not exist — written to `/output/entry.txt` by `main.py`.

> Good morning, Breathton: minus 4°, frost on the platform — your breath fogs the bus windows, where the driver has drawn you a sunny day.

136 characters. Breathton is invented; the forecast is real broadcast weather, bent just enough for a bored commuter to smile at the bus stop.
